# ENTERPRISE HR LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE POLICY (2026)
Organization: Enterprise Global Solutions Inc.
Document ID: POL-HR-2026-001
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applicability: All Full-Time and Part-Time Employees

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This policy outlines the guidelines, entitlements, and procedures regarding employee leaves, attendance tracking, and work schedules. Enterprise Global Solutions Inc. is committed to supporting work-life balance while ensuring operational continuity.

2. ANNUAL PAID LEAVE ENTITLEMENT
- Employees are entitled to 18 paid leaves annually (accrued at the rate of 1.5 days per completed calendar month).
- Paid leaves can be utilized for vacations, personal obligations, or rest.
- A maximum of 8 unused paid leaves can be carried forward to the following calendar year. Any remaining balance beyond 8 days will lapse at year-end.
- Leave requests exceeding 3 consecutive business days require advance notice of at least 7 days submitted through the company portal.

3. SICK & MEDICAL LEAVE
- Full-time employees are provided with 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
- Sick leave applies to personal illness, medical appointments, or caretaking of immediate family members.
- For medical absences exceeding 3 consecutive workdays, a certified medical certificate from a licensed physician must be uploaded to the HR portal upon return.

4. CASUAL & EMERGENCY LEAVE
- Employees are granted 6 days of casual leave per calendar year for unforeseen personal emergencies or urgent family matters.
- Casual leaves cannot be clubbed with more than 3 consecutive days of annual paid leave without prior managerial approval.

5. PARENTAL & SPECIAL LEAVES
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks of fully paid leave for eligible birth mothers.
- Paternity Leave: 4 weeks of fully paid leave for new fathers and adoptive parents.
- Bereavement Leave: Up to 5 consecutive paid working days in the event of the loss of an immediate family member.

6. ATTENDANCE & CORE WORKING HOURS
- Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday, with a 60-minute lunch break.
- Flexible core hours allow starting between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM, provided the total 8 working hours per day are fulfilled.
- Clock-in and attendance records are managed through the internal employee portal.

7. APPROVAL WORKFLOW
- All planned leaves must be submitted via the Enterprise Assistant or employee self-service portal.
- Line managers are required to review and approve or reject leave submissions within 48 business hours.
