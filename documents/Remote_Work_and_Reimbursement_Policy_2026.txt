# REMOTE WORK AND EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY (2026)
Organization: Enterprise Global Solutions Inc.
Document ID: POL-FIN-2026-004
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applicability: All Eligible Remote & Hybrid Employees

1. OBJECTIVE
This document establishes the official company policies regarding hybrid working arrangements, ad-hoc remote work days, and eligible expense reimbursements.

2. WORK-FROM-HOME (WFH) & HYBRID MODEL
- Eligible team members can work remotely up to two (2) days per calendar week upon agreement with their department lead.
- Employees wishing to work remotely on designated in-office days (such as next Monday or specific sprint planning days) must submit a formal Remote Work Request at least 24 hours in advance.
- The request must specify the date, reason, and confirmation of stable high-speed internet and security compliance.
- The AI Assistant is authorized to log remote work requests directly into the HR system on behalf of authenticated employees.

3. HOME OFFICE ERGONOMIC SETUP ALLOWANCE
- Newly hired full-time employees or employees transitioning to hybrid status are eligible for a one-time home office equipment stipend of up to $500.
- Eligible items include ergonomic desk chairs, external monitors, laptop docking stations, ergonomic keyboards, and noise-cancelling headsets.
- Receipts and proof of purchase must be submitted within 60 days of purchase through the expense portal.

4. MONTHLY INTERNET & CONNECTIVITY SUBSIDY
- Active remote and hybrid staff are entitled to a monthly internet connectivity reimbursement of up to $50 per month.
- Claims must be filed quarterly or monthly with a copy of the ISP billing statement.

5. TRAVEL & MEAL REIMBURSEMENTS
- Travel for official client meetings or company retreats must be pre-approved by the department manager.
- Daily meal allowance during approved travel: Breakfast up to $20, Lunch up to $30, Dinner up to $50. Alcohol expenses are strictly non-reimbursable.
- Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $200 per night for domestic travel.
